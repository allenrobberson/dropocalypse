## Description
Dropocalypse brings back the music visualizers you loved from the Winamp and MilkDrop days, and puts your own logo in the middle of the show.

Play music from Spotify, YouTube, a DJ app, a microphone or a line-in, and Dropocalypse turns it into full-screen, GPU-powered visuals. When the bass hits, your logo bounces, squashes, slides and glows right on the beat.

PERFECT FOR
• DJs and live shows: put your brand on the big screen behind you
• Streamers: a reactive backdrop with your channel logo
• Bars, venues and parties
• Anyone who misses the classic Winamp visuals

FEATURES
• 26 visual styles, including Classic Tunnel, Spectrum Skyline, Liquid Plasma, Black Hole, Outrun Grid, Hyperspace, Digital Rain, LED Meter, a 4D Tesseract, Full Kaleidoscope and Main Stage
• Your logo reacts to the music: pulse, squash, bounce, slide, wobble, glow and trails, each adjustable
• Up to 4 logos at once, plus text such as a DJ name, an event or a hashtag
• Listens to whatever your PC is playing (no microphone needed), one output device, a microphone or a line-in
• Two screens: detach the controls onto your laptop and send the visuals full screen to a TV or projector
• Record the visuals and the music to an MP4 video (720p, 1080p or 4K, landscape or 9:16 portrait)
• Auto-cycle through the styles you pick, from 5 seconds to 6 minutes each
• Low-latency audio analysis so the visuals land on the beat

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Audio is analyzed live on your PC and never sent anywhere; it's only saved when you press Record, and only to your own computer. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Dropocalypse is free and open source under the MIT license. Source code, guide and community: github.com/allenrobberson/dropocalypse

Note: Dropocalypse shows rapidly changing, flashing visuals. Viewer discretion is advised for people with photosensitive epilepsy.
