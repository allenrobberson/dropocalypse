## Description
Dropocalypse brings back the music visualizers you loved from the Winamp and MilkDrop days, and puts your own logo in the middle of the show.

Play music from Spotify, Apple Music, a DJ app, a microphone or a line-in, and Dropocalypse turns it into full-screen, GPU-powered visuals. When the bass hits, your logo bounces, squashes, slides and glows right on the beat.

PERFECT FOR
• DJs and live shows: put your brand on the big screen behind you
• Streamers: a reactive backdrop with your channel logo
• Bars, venues and parties
• Anyone who misses the classic Winamp visuals

FEATURES
• 25 visual styles, including Classic Tunnel, Spectrum Skyline, Liquid Plasma, Black Hole, Outrun Grid, Hyperspace, Digital Rain, LED Meter, a 4D Tesseract and Full Kaleidoscope
• Your logo reacts to the music: pulse, squash, bounce, slide, wobble, glow and trails, each adjustable
• Listen to any app on your Mac (macOS 14.2 or later), all Mac audio, a microphone or a line-in
• Low-latency audio analysis so the visuals land on the beat
• Auto-cycle through the styles you pick
• True full screen on any display, with a toolbar that hides itself
• Works with PNG, JPEG, HEIC, PDF and SVG logos (transparent PNGs look best)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Audio is analyzed live on your Mac and is never recorded or sent anywhere. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Dropocalypse is free and open source under the MIT license. Source code, guide and community: github.com/allenrobberson/dropocalypse

Note: Dropocalypse shows rapidly changing, flashing visuals. Viewer discretion is advised for people with photosensitive epilepsy.
