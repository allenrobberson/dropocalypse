# Quick Start

1. Open **Dropocalypse**, click **Audio** in the toolbar and pick the app you're playing music from (for example **Spotify**), or a mic or line-in. Allow access when macOS asks.
2. Play some music. The visuals start reacting right away.
3. Drag your logo (PNG, JPG, PDF or SVG) onto the window, or press **L** to pick a file.
4. Press **F** for full screen.

Your logo, style and all settings are saved automatically and come back the next time you open the app.

## What you're looking at

- The **visuals** fill the window and react to the music in real time.
- Your **logo** (or the "YOUR LOGO" placeholder) sits in the middle and reacts to the bass.
- The **toolbar** along the top edge hides itself after a few seconds. Move the mouse to the top, click anywhere, or press **T** to bring it back.

## Five things to try next

1. Click the **style name** in the toolbar and pick a different look, or press **←** and **→**.
2. Turn on **auto-cycle** (⟳) and uncheck any styles you don't want in the rotation. See [[Styles]].
3. Open **Motion** and push **Bounce** and **Squash** up for a wilder logo. See [[Your Logo and Motion]].
4. Open **Logo** and turn on **Logo trails** so your logo melts into the visuals.
5. Press **F** for edge-to-edge full screen, then **Esc** twice to come back.
