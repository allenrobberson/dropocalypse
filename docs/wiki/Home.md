# Dropocalypse

**The drop hits. Your logo goes off.**

Make your logo dance to the beat. **Free music visualizer** for DJs, streamers & venues. Record clips for **TikTok, Reels & YouTube**. Mac, Windows & Linux.

Dropocalypse is a free, open-source, Winamp / MilkDrop-style music visualizer for **Mac, Windows and Linux** that makes **your logo dance to the bass**. Play music and the visuals swirl, zoom and pulse while your logo pulses, squashes and stretches, bounces, slides and wobbles on every kick drum.

## Highlights

| Feature | What you get |
|---|---|
| 🎛 **26 styles** | Winamp/MilkDrop-style originals, fan-favorite looks (bass ring, neon equalizer, synthwave grid, hyperspace…), three more equalizers, a 4D Tesseract, Full Kaleidoscope and Main Stage. See [[Styles]]. |
| 🖼 **Your logos and text** | Up to 4 at once, side by side or stacked. Drag images onto the window or add text in your font and color. They react to the bass with five separate motions plus glow, tint and trails. See [[Your Logo and Motion]]. |
| 🎧 **No mic needed** | Listen straight to Spotify or any app on a Mac (macOS 14.2+), or to whatever your computer is playing on Windows and Linux. Mics and line-in work too. See [[Audio Sources]]. |
| ⚡ **Low latency** | About 10–20 ms from sound to screen, so drops land on the beat. See [[How It Works]]. |
| 🔴 **Record clips** | Save the visuals and the music as an MP4 (720p, 1080p or 4K) for TikTok, Reels and YouTube, in landscape or a vertical 9:16 frame. See [[Controls and Settings]]. |
| 🖥 **Built for shows** | Edge-to-edge full screen, an auto-hiding toolbar, and auto-cycle through the styles you pick. See [[Live Shows and TVs]]. |
| 💸 **Free forever** | No account, no ads, no tracking, no watermark. MIT licensed. The Windows version is portable and needs no admin rights, or [get it from the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N1TJ9D5R5C8). |
