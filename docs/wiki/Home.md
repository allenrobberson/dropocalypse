# Dropocalypse

**The drop hits. Your logo goes off.**

Dropocalypse is a free, open-source, Winamp / MilkDrop-style music visualizer for **Mac and Windows** that makes **your logo dance to the bass**. Play music and the visuals swirl, zoom and pulse while your logo pulses, squashes and stretches, bounces, slides and wobbles on every kick drum.

**[⬇️ Download the latest version](https://github.com/allenrobberson/dropocalypse/releases/latest)** · [📖 Printable PDF guide](https://github.com/allenrobberson/dropocalypse/blob/main/docs/Dropocalypse-User-Guide.pdf) · [💬 Discussions](https://github.com/allenrobberson/dropocalypse/discussions) · [❤️ Sponsor](https://github.com/sponsors/allenrobberson)

## Highlights

| Feature | What you get |
|---|---|
| 🎛 **24 styles** | Winamp/MilkDrop-style originals, fan-favorite looks (bass ring, neon equalizer, synthwave grid, hyperspace…), three more equalizers and a 4D Tesseract. See [[Styles]]. |
| 🖼 **Your logo** | Drag any image onto the window. It reacts to the bass with five separate motions plus glow, tint and trails. See [[Your Logo and Motion]]. |
| 🎧 **No mic needed** | Listen straight to Spotify or any app on a Mac (macOS 14.2+), or to whatever your PC is playing on Windows. Mics and line-in work too. See [[Audio Sources]]. |
| ⚡ **Low latency** | About 10–20 ms from sound to screen, so drops land on the beat. See [[How It Works]]. |
| 🖥 **Built for shows** | Edge-to-edge full screen, an auto-hiding toolbar, and auto-cycle through the styles you pick. See [[Live Shows and TVs]]. |
| 💸 **Free forever** | No account, no ads, no tracking, no watermark. MIT licensed. The Windows version is portable and needs no admin rights. |
