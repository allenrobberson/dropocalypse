# Live Shows and TVs

## Setting up for a live show

1. **Playing from the same computer?** On a Mac, pick your music or DJ app (or *All Mac audio*) under **Audio → Listen to**. On Windows, pick *System audio*. It's the cleanest signal there is, with no cables or mic.
2. **Use a direct feed if you can.** Run the booth, record or aux output of your mixer or DJ controller into a USB audio interface, then pick that interface under **Audio**. A direct feed reacts more cleanly than a mic hearing a loud room.
3. **Using a mic instead?** Point it toward the speakers or subwoofer. Raise **Sensitivity** if the room is quiet; lower it if crowd noise is driving the visuals.
4. **Tune the beat.** Watch the logo during a track with a clear kick. If it misses kicks, lower **Beat threshold**. If it jumps on hi-hats or vocals, raise it.
5. **Go full screen** on the projector or TV: drag the window onto that screen and press **F**. In landscape, full screen covers every pixel, edge to edge: the Mac's menu bar and Dock are hidden, and on MacBooks with a camera notch the visuals fill the strip beside the notch too. In portrait, full screen centers the 9:16 frame and leaves the sides black.
6. Turn **auto-cycle** on with 30 to 60 seconds per style so the show keeps changing without you touching it. Uncheck any styles in the style list that don't fit the night.

## Two screens: controls on one, visuals on the other

1. Click **Detach** in the toolbar (or press **D**). The controls move into their own **Dropocalypse Controls** window, stacked top to bottom, with every panel shown in place.
2. Under **Visuals**, pick your TV, projector or second monitor from the screen list. The visuals go full screen there.
3. Keep the controls window on your main screen and change styles, logo, motion and audio while the show runs. Your audience only sees the visuals.
4. Click **Attach controls to the visuals** (or close the controls window) to put the toolbar back on top. Dropocalypse remembers which way you left it.

## Showing it on a TV or projector

| Method | Lag | Quality | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HDMI cable** | None | Best | Plug in, then pick the TV in the **Screen** list (or drag the window onto it and press **F**). Detach the controls to keep them on your laptop. The best choice for live shows. |
| **AirPlay** (Mac) | Low | Good | Control Center → **Screen Mirroring** → your Apple TV or AirPlay TV (many Samsung, LG, Sony and Vizio sets) |
| **Miracast** (Windows) | Low | Good | Press **Win + K** and pick a Miracast TV or adapter |
| **Chromecast** | Some | OK | In Chrome: **⋮ menu → Cast… → Sources → Cast screen**, pick your TV, then run Dropocalypse full screen |

Dropocalypse doesn't have Chromecast built in. Google's Cast toolkit is made for phones and web pages, not desktop apps, and streaming the visuals as video would put the TV a few seconds behind the music. Chrome's screen casting works today with a short delay.

## Show checklist

- [ ] Audio source picked and reacting (status line shows the input)
- [ ] Beat threshold tuned on a track with a clear kick
- [ ] Logo added and sized for the room
- [ ] Auto-cycle on, unwanted styles unchecked, timer set (30–60 s)
- [ ] Brightness adjusted for the projector
- [ ] Full screen on the right display (**F**)
- [ ] Computer set not to sleep (Mac: System Settings → Lock Screen / Displays; Windows: Power settings)
