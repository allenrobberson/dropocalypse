# Support the Project

Dropocalypse is free and always will be. If it livens up your shows, streams or parties, here's how you can help.

## ❤️ Sponsor on GitHub

**[github.com/sponsors/allenrobberson](https://github.com/sponsors/allenrobberson)**

Sponsorships go to the developer and keep the project going. They cover the yearly Apple Developer membership that keeps Mac downloads signed and notarized, a Windows code-signing certificate, and time for new styles and features.

## Other ways to help

- ⭐ **Star the repository** so more people find it.
- 📣 **Share a clip** of your logo in action on Reddit, Instagram or TikTok, and post it in [Discussions](https://github.com/allenrobberson/dropocalypse/discussions).
- 🐞 **Report bugs** in [Issues](https://github.com/allenrobberson/dropocalypse/issues), with your OS, version and audio source.
- 💡 **Suggest styles** in Discussions, or build one yourself: see [[Adding a Style]].
- 🧪 **Test on your hardware.** Reports from Windows PCs, Linux desktops, Intel Macs and audio interfaces are especially helpful.
