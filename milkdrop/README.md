# MilkDrop presets

Dropocalypse can play MilkDrop presets (`.milk` files) as styles, using the open-source
[projectM](https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/projectm) engine.

## Download

**[Dropocalypse-MilkDrop-Presets.zip](Dropocalypse-MilkDrop-Presets.zip?raw=1)** has the 9 presets
that come with Dropocalypse, the texture they use, and the credits for their authors. The same
files are in [presets](presets) and [textures](textures).

Thousands more presets are free online. A great place to start is the "Cream of the Crop"
collection (about 9,800 presets, sorted into folders with preview pictures) from
[Jason Fletcher (Isosceles)](https://www.jasonfletcher.info/vjloops).

## Use them in Dropocalypse

1. Unzip the presets anywhere on your computer.
2. In Dropocalypse, click the style name to open the style list.
3. Under **Your MilkDrop presets**, click **Choose folder** and pick the folder with the `.milk`
   files. Subfolders are searched too.
4. Click a preset to show it. Turn on **Include these presets in auto-cycle** if you want them in
   the rotation.

If a preset uses textures, keep its collection's `Textures` folder next to the presets folder
(as it comes in MilkDrop and NestDrop downloads) and Dropocalypse finds them.

See [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) for who made each bundled preset.
