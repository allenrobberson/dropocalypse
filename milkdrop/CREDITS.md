# MilkDrop presets in Dropocalypse

Dropocalypse plays MilkDrop presets with **projectM** (https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/projectm),
the open-source MilkDrop engine, used under the GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1. projectM is
shipped as its own library file beside the app (projectM-4.dll, libprojectM-4.so or
libprojectM-4.dylib), so it can be replaced with any compatible build.

MilkDrop was created by Ryan Geiss. The presets below were written by the MilkDrop community and
come from the "Cream of the Crop" collection curated by Jason Fletcher (Isosceles),
https://www.jasonfletcher.info/vjloops. All credit for them goes to their authors.

## Your own presets

Any `.milk` file works. In Dropocalypse, open the style list and use **Your MilkDrop presets >
Choose folder...** to pick a folder of presets (subfolders are searched too). If a `Textures` folder
sits next to the presets, as in MilkDrop and NestDrop installs, the presets find their textures there.
