DROPOCALYPSE for Windows (portable)
The drop hits. Your logo goes off.
https://github.com/allenrobberson/dropocalypse

RUN IT
  Double-click Dropocalypse.exe. Nothing is installed and no admin rights are needed.
  You can keep this folder anywhere: Desktop, Documents or a USB stick.

  If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway".
  (This happens because the free app isn't code-signed.)

QUICK START
  1. Play some music on this PC. Dropocalypse listens to what the PC is playing by default.
  2. Drag your logo image onto the window, or press L.
  3. Press F for full screen.

  Move the mouse to the top edge to show the toolbar. It hides itself after a few seconds.

KEYS
  F / F11        Full screen
  T / S          Show / hide toolbar
  Esc            Hide toolbar / exit full screen
  L  (Ctrl+O)    Choose logo
  G              Show / hide logo
  Left / Right   Previous / next style
  A              Auto-cycle on / off

AUDIO
  Toolbar > Audio lets you pick:
    - System audio (what this PC is playing): no microphone needed
    - Any microphone, USB interface or line-in
    - "Playing on: ..." to listen to a specific output device
  Using a microphone? Turn on Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone >
  "Let desktop apps access your microphone".

WHERE THINGS ARE SAVED
  Settings and your logo: %APPDATA%\Dropocalypse
  To remove Dropocalypse completely, delete this folder and that one.
